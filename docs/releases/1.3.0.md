# Popeye G&W 1.3.0

Published and verified on 6 October 2026. The owner reported that the 1.1–1.2
beeps were too loud and said they were happy with the new levels. The frozen
candidate was installed on the owner's Pebble Time 2, and the owner replied,
"I approve 887df5fc…eddeb for GitHub v1.3.0 and the RePebble listing." No
detailed play-test observations were reported.

## Changes

- Make the beeps quieter and add volume levels: Settings → Sound now cycles Off, Low, Medium and High. Medium, the new default, is a third of the previous level, and High is still quieter than before. The alarm uses the same level. Existing On/Off choices are kept.

Speaker volumes (0–100): Low 10, Medium 20, High 35. 1.1.0–1.2.0 played every
cue at 60. Settings record v8 stores the level in the record's last two spare
bits; v1–v7 records load with Sound at Medium when it was On.

Before this release, `main` CI had failed since the Clock 1.0.0 publication
record. Two upload-tool tests assumed the clock had no store ID. They were made
independent of registration state in `bfc56a0`, a tests-only commit. The game
commits were rebased onto the Clock release before preparation. The frozen PBW,
manifest and logs are retained locally under `.release/1.3.0/`. No rebuild
followed the freeze.

## Publication

- [GitHub release](https://github.com/ewijaya/popeye-gw/releases/tag/v1.3.0): published as Latest. Clock 1.0.0 is unchanged. The downloaded PBW matches the approved SHA-256.
- [Pebble App Store](https://apps.repebble.com/e9cb2950ca21440798fb1db8): published. The general and Emery catalogs show 1.3.0, the exact frozen notes, Emery compatibility and matching PBW bytes.

The store upload used `tools/upload_store.py --app popeye-gw` with one release
POST. The Dashboard read-back confirmed the release and that the listing's
metadata, description, artwork and earlier releases were preserved.
