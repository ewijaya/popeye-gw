Popeye G&W brings the charm of 1981 LCD handheld games to Pebble Time 2. Catch Olive Oyl's flying food, dodge Brutus and chase your best score in Game A or B. Enjoy vivid scenery, portrait or landscape play, an animated clock, a daily alarm and saved high scores. Brief vibration cues respect Quiet Time. Works on the watch without a phone connection. A fan-made adaptation inspired by Nintendo's Popeye Wide Screen PP-23, with original generated artwork and provisional gameplay timing.
