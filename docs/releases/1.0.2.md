# Popeye G&W 1.0.2

Published and verified on 6 October 2026 after the owner tested the installed
frozen candidate and replied, "Everything looks good! Approved!" No additional
detailed physical-watch observations were supplied.

## Changes

- Keep the clock and score digits at their original proportions in Horizontal mode, with more space around the display edges.
- Align the faint digit and MISS outlines with the updated layout for both Top and Bottom button positions.
- Preserve the Vertical layout and existing gameplay, settings, alarms and high scores.

clean isolated checkout of the preparation commit. An unrelated local deletion
of `PRD.md` was excluded and left untouched. The PRD remains in the release.

under `.release/1.0.2/`. The PT2 installation used that exact frozen PBW and
reported success before the owner's approval. No rebuild followed the freeze.
