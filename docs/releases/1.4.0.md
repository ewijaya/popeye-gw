# Popeye G&W 1.4.0

Published and verified on 7 October 2026. The frozen candidate was installed on
the owner's Pebble Time 2, and the owner replied, "I approve 89788e69…e93a for
GitHub v1.4.0 and the game listing, and b1191bcb…c911 for GitHub clock-v1.0.2
and the Clock listing." No detailed play-test observations were reported.

## Changes

- Olive throws Popeye a kiss when you set a new best: after the last miss, a heart flies from her to Popeye before the Game over box appears. Any button skips it. At the 200 and 500 bonuses she blows a quick kiss from her ledge.
- On the clock, Olive kisses Popeye at the top of every hour, and all day on 14 February she throws hearts instead of food.
- The second bottle (lane 2) is now a barrel, so all four foods look different.

The kiss pose is variant A of three generated from `art/source/olive.png`, approved
by the owner. Hearts are pixel art in `tools/prepare_art.py`. The segment inventory
grew from 82 to 89. Game rules, timing, scoring and fairness are unchanged.
