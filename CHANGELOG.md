# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-05

A whole childhood on one small screen. The first release of Popeye G&W brings
Olive Oyl, Popeye and Brutus to Pebble Time 2 in a vivid LCD-style harbour.

- Play Game A or B, catch food, dodge Brutus and keep a separate best score for each mode.
- Enjoy an animated clock and a daily alarm that can wake the app while it is closed.
- Play upright or sideways, with landscape buttons below or above the screen; Bottom is the first landscape choice.
- Use large menus and three short Help screens. Adjust swapped controls, vibration, ghost segments and the clock demonstration.
- Keep scores and settings across app updates. Vibration respects Quiet Time.

An unofficial adaptation of the 1981 Popeye Wide Screen PP-23 for Pebble Time 2
(Emery). Original handheld timing is not reproduced exactly. No phone connection
is needed during play.
