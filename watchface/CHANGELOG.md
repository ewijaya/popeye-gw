# Popeye G&W Clock changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-06

Old-school charm. Right on time. The first release brings Popeye, Olive Oyl
and Brutus to an animated LCD watchface inspired by 1980s handhelds.

- Choose Classic, Everyday, Traveller or Large Time layouts, with independent animation activity controls.
- Customize two information rows: date, battery, steps, optional weather, world time or an event countdown.
- Enjoy Ivory, Original, Green LCD and Amber themes, subtle ghost segments, larger digits and high contrast.
- Read clear information text aligned with the clock, with Japanese date support and four battery display styles.
- Configure everything through the phone's My Apps settings. Saved preferences stay with the watchface.
- Motion pauses while covered, during Quiet Time or configured quiet hours, and at your chosen low-battery cutoff. Sound is off; disconnect vibration is optional and off by default.

Weather requires opt-in and a phone Internet connection; steps require Pebble
Health access. Emery only. An unofficial watchface with no official affiliation;
the playable Popeye G&W game is a separate app.
