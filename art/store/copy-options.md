# Banner copy review

Current approved copy: **Popeye G&W**, **BACK TO GAME & WATCH**, and
**A whole childhood on one small screen.** The footer uses large vintage
lettering without decorative lines.

The initial pairs below are retained as the review record. The owner's latest direction is to evoke the original Nintendo
Game & Watch, rather than a generic retro game, without naming a specific
Pebble model or using “1981-inspired • Fan-made” on the banner.
